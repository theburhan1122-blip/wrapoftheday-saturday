McDonald's Wrap of the Day Saturday: Calories & Nutrition Guide

Planning your Saturday McDonald's visit but unsure which wrap is available? Many customers visit their local restaurant only to find out they missed the wrap they wanted. Not knowing the Saturday wrap deal can mean paying full price or missing out on a tasty meal.

Good news! This guide covers everything about McDonald's Wrap of the Day Saturday in the UK. You will learn about the Tikka Chicken Wrap, its calories, nutrition facts, ingredients, and allergens. Keep reading to make your Saturday lunch both delicious and budget friendly.

What Is the Wrap of the Day on Saturday?

On Saturdays, McDonald's UK serves the Tikka Chicken Wrap as its Wrap of the Day. This wrap comes in two versions: Crispy and Grilled. Both options are available at a discounted price when you order in the restaurant or through the app.

The Tikka Chicken Wrap features tender chicken with tikka mayo, mango and lime chutney, fresh onions, lettuce, and cucumber. Everything is wrapped in a soft, toasted tortilla. This wrap is also available on Wednesday, so you get two chances each week to enjoy it.

Note: Please note that the Wrap of the Day discount is not available on delivery orders.

McDonald's Wrap of the Day Saturday: Crispy vs Grilled

Both versions of the Tikka Chicken Wrap taste great. However, they differ in calories, fat content, and protein. Here is a quick comparison to help you choose.

Key Ingredients Explained

Chicken Options

Crispy Chicken Select contains chicken breast meat (57%), wheat flour, vegetable oils, and various seasonings. It provides a crunchy texture and satisfying bite.

Grilled Chicken contains chicken breast meat, potato starch, natural flavourings, salt, and brown sugar. It offers a leaner, more protein packed option.

Tikka Mayo

This creamy sauce uses rapeseed oil, tandoori spice marinade, and free range egg yolk. The marinade includes garlic, ginger, turmeric, cumin, and other warming spices. This sauce gives the wrap its signature Indian inspired flavour.

Mango and Lime Chutney

Made with 46% mango, lime juice concentrate, and aromatic spices like coriander and cumin. This chutney adds a sweet and tangy contrast to the savoury chicken.

Tortilla

The soft tortilla contains wheat flour, wholemeal wheat flour, and rapeseed oil. It is toasted fresh in the restaurant.

Allergen Information

Both wrap versions contain several allergens. Review this list before ordering.

Contains:

  • Wheat (gluten)
  • Celery
  • Egg
  • Mustard

May Contain:

  • Milk
  • Sesame seeds
  • Barley
  • Rye

McDonald's prepares all food in shared kitchens where allergens are present. They cannot guarantee any meal is completely allergen free. If you have severe allergies, speak with staff before ordering.

There are no nut or peanut ingredients in these wraps. However, McDonald's cannot guarantee the food is completely nut free due to shared preparation areas.

Tips for a Healthier McDonald's Saturday Visit

  • Choose grilled over crispy to save 129 calories and reduce fat intake by more than half.
  • Skip the fries and add a side salad for a more balanced meal.
  • Drink water instead of sugary drinks to keep your calorie count low.
  • Check the app for additional deals that can be combined with your order.
  • Order in restaurant to get the Wrap of the Day discount since delivery orders do not qualify.

Is the Tikka Chicken Wrap Worth It?

The McDonald's Wrap of the Day Saturday offers great value. You get a flavourful meal with quality ingredients at a reduced price. The tikka mayo and mango lime chutney create a unique taste you will not find in other fast food wraps.

The grilled version is excellent for those watching their calories. It delivers 29 grams of protein, which is 59% of your daily reference intake. This makes it a solid post workout meal or satisfying lunch option.

The crispy version works well when you want something more indulgent. At 471 calories, it still fits within most daily calorie budgets.

Frequently Asked Questions

What wrap is available on Saturday at McDonald's UK?
The Tikka Chicken Wrap is the Wrap of the Day on Saturday. You can choose between crispy or grilled chicken versions.
How many calories are in the McDonald's Saturday wrap?
The crispy version has 471 calories. The grilled version has 342 calories. Choose grilled for a lighter option.
Can I get the Wrap of the Day discount on delivery?
No. The Wrap of the Day discount only applies to orders placed in restaurant or through the McDonald's app for collection.
Is the Tikka Chicken Wrap spicy?
The wrap has mild warmth from the tikka spices. It is not overly spicy and suits most taste preferences.
What allergens are in the Tikka Chicken Wrap?
The wrap contains wheat, celery, egg, and mustard. It may also contain milk, sesame, barley, and rye.
Which days can I get the Tikka Chicken Wrap?
The Tikka Chicken Wrap is available as Wrap of the Day on both Wednesday and Saturday.
